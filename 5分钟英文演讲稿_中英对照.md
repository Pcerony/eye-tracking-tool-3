# 植物园解说标识研究：英文演讲稿

> 对应当前 `deck-manifest.json` 中的 16 个 Slide  
> 英文难度：托福 70 分左右  
> 建议时长：约 5–6 分钟  
> 第 5 页是重点页。演讲时只读英文，中文用于理解和练习。

## Slide 1 — Research Title

**English**

Good afternoon, everyone. My name is Pingcheng Wang from Kyushu University. Today, I will present my research on co-creation tools for designing interpretive signs. My research site is the greenhouse area of Fukuoka City Botanical Garden.

**中文翻译**

大家下午好。我是来自九州大学的王平成。今天，我将介绍关于解说标识设计共创工具的研究。我的研究地点是福冈市植物园的温室区域。

## Slide 2 — Botanical Gardens and Plant Learning

**English**

More than 60 percent of visitors come to botanical gardens to learn and explore, not only to enjoy the scenery. In a greenhouse, interpretive signs are an important way for visitors to receive plant knowledge.

**中文翻译**

超过 60% 的游客来到植物园，是为了学习和探索，而不只是欣赏风景。在温室中，解说标识是游客获取植物知识的重要方式。

## Slide 3 — Low Reading Rate and Co-creation

**English**

However, visitors often read only a small part of a sign. Only about 20 percent deeply read long content. This means the first step of learning already has a serious problem. Botanical gardens therefore use co-creation with plant experts, staff, citizens, and designers to make signs more attractive.

**中文翻译**

然而，游客通常只阅读标识内容的一小部分。大约只有 20% 的游客会深入阅读长篇内容。这说明学习流程的第一步已经出现了严重问题。因此，植物园会让植物专家、工作人员、市民和设计师参与共创，使标识更有吸引力。

## Slide 4 — Research Gaps and Objectives

**English**

However, there are two research gaps in the co-creation process. First, there is no clear, evidence-based principle for selecting problems and guiding design ideas. Second, after a sign is designed, there is no objective tool for measuring attention and reading coverage. Therefore, this study aims to build attention-based A/R/S principles and develop both qualitative and quantitative diagnostic tools.

**中文翻译**

然而，共创设计流程中存在两个研究缺口。第一，缺少有证据支持的设计原则来筛选问题并指导创意。第二，标识设计完成后，缺少测量注意力和完读率的客观工具。因此，本研究旨在建立注意力导向的 A/R/S 原则，并开发定性与定量诊断工具。

## Slide 5 — Research Process

**English**

This slide shows the whole research process. It connects theory, methods, and practice through two objectives and four steps. Objective One is to build attention-based design principles. In Step 1, experts and designers use literature reviews, field research, sign audits, and interviews. The output is a clear research gap and theoretical framework. In Step 2, visitors, garden staff, plant experts, and designers take part in workshops, interviews, surveys, and KJ analysis. Their ideas are organized into three principles: Atmosphere, Relevance, and Structure, or A/R/S.

Objective Two is to develop objective tools for co-creation. In Step 3, experts and designers develop Signage Annotator. It supports sign-image coding and produces a thematic analysis report. In Step 4, visitors and designers use SIGN Visual Attention v3. It records gaze without touching the participant and produces heatmaps, gaze-path images, and feedback. Together, the two tools connect qualitative co-creation ideas with quantitative evidence. The results can then return to the design process and support the next improvement.

**中文翻译**

这一页展示了完整的研究流程，通过两个目标和四个步骤连接理论、方法与实践。目标一是建立注意力导向的设计原则。步骤一由专家和设计师开展文献综述、实地调查、标识审计和访谈，产出明确的研究缺口与理论框架。步骤二邀请游客、植物园工作人员、植物专家和设计师参与工作坊、访谈、问卷与 KJ 法分析，将多方意见整理为启发性氛围、观众关联性和结构清晰性三个原则，也就是 A/R/S。

目标二是为共创流程开发客观工具。步骤三由专家和设计师开发 Signage Annotator，用于标识图像编码并生成主题分析报告。步骤四由游客和设计师使用 SIGN Visual Attention v3。该工具能够以非接触方式记录视线，并生成热力图、注视点路径图和反馈。两项工具共同把定性的共创意见与定量证据连接起来，分析结果还可以返回设计流程，支持下一轮改良。

## Slide 6 — Theory and Co-creation Research

**English**

In Phase 1, the literature review showed three key ideas. Interpretation should connect with personal experience, inspire visitors, and use a clear structure. In Phase 2, I conducted field research, interviews, and surveys with visitors, garden staff, plant experts, and designers.

**中文翻译**

在阶段一，文献综述提出了三个关键观点：解说内容应该联系个人经验、启发游客，并采用清楚的结构。在阶段二，我与游客、植物园工作人员、植物专家和设计师进行了实地调查、访谈与问卷调查。

## Slide 7 — A/R/S Design Principles

**English**

The first research outcome is the A/R/S framework. A means Atmosphere. It uses questions or emotional language to start attention. R means Relevance. It connects plants with sensory and daily experience. S means Structure. It uses sections and visual levels to reduce information load.

**中文翻译**

第一个研究成果是 A/R/S 框架。A 代表启发性氛围，通过问题或情感语言启动注意力。R 代表观众关联性，将植物与感官和日常经验联系起来。S 代表结构清晰性，通过分区和视觉层级降低信息负担。

## Slide 8 — Signage Annotator

**English**

Next, I developed Signage Annotator for experts and designers. The tool imports sign images, supports custom areas and coding rules, allows shared and repeated coding, and produces several kinds of quantitative analysis. I used it to study 70 greenhouse signs and 182 design features.

**中文翻译**

接下来，我为专家和设计师开发了 Signage Annotator。该工具可以导入标识图像，支持自定义分区和编码规则、多人共享与重复编码，并生成多种定量分析。我用它分析了 70 块温室标识和 182 个设计特征。

## Slide 9 — Coding Results

**English**

The coding results show 41 Atmosphere features, 47 Relevance features, and 94 Structure features. Question-based A1 features made up only about 15 percent. Different greenhouse areas had different gaps, but the overall results clearly showed the need for more Atmosphere and Relevance features.

**中文翻译**

编码结果包括 41 个启发性氛围特征、47 个观众关联性特征和 94 个结构特征。提问式 A1 特征只占约 15%。不同温室区域存在不同缺失，但整体结果清楚地说明需要增加 A 和 R 特征。

## Slide 10 — Experimental Design and Tool

**English**

For the experiment, I compared a traditional sign with an A/R improved sign. I also used SIGN Visual Attention, which works with a normal web camera and open-source methods. It tracks gaze without contact and maps the gaze onto a printed sign. It can then create heatmaps and gaze-path images.

**中文翻译**

在实验中，我比较了传统标识和经过 A/R 改良的标识。我还使用了 SIGN Visual Attention。它通过普通网络摄像头和开源方法工作，能够以非接触方式追踪视线，并把视线映射到纸质标识上，之后生成热力图和注视点路径图。

## Slide 11 — Eye-tracking Procedure

**English**

Fifteen visitors from young, middle-aged, and older groups joined the test. First, we checked the light, face tracking, and camera. Second, we completed a nine-point physical calibration. Third, participants read naturally while the system recorded their gaze. Finally, we exported and cleaned the data.

**中文翻译**

共有 15 名来自青年、壮年和老年群体的游客参加测试。首先，我们检查光照、面部捕捉和摄像头。其次，完成九点物理空间校准。然后，参与者自然阅读，系统同时记录视线。最后，我们导出并清洗数据。

## Slide 12 — Heatmap Overview

**English**

These heatmaps compare the control and intervention groups. Warm colors show stronger attention, and cool colors show weaker attention. The patterns help us see how the two designs changed the location and strength of visitors' attention.

**中文翻译**

这些热力图比较了对照组和实验组。暖色表示较强注意力，冷色表示较弱注意力。这些图像帮助我们观察两种设计如何改变游客注意力的位置和强度。

## Slide 13 — Main Results

**English**

The first result is reading coverage. During the first ten seconds, it increased from 38.6 percent to 45.3 percent, a gain of 6.7 percentage points. The second result is Relevance. Attention to the R area increased from 5.0 to 21.4 percent in the first ten seconds, and from 8.5 to 35.1 percent during the whole reading time. All nine valid samples showed the same positive direction.

**中文翻译**

第一项结果是阅读覆盖率。在最初十秒内，完读率从 38.6% 提高到 45.3%，增加了 6.7 个百分点。第二项结果是观众关联性。R 区域的注意力占比在最初十秒内从 5.0% 提高到 21.4%，在整个阅读期间从 8.5% 提高到 35.1%。九个有效样本都表现出了相同的正向趋势。

## Slide 14 — Conclusions

**English**

This study has two main findings. First, both tools were developed and showed practical usability in the research process. Second, the A/R/S principles were proposed and tested. R showed both necessity and effectiveness. A did not yet show clear effectiveness, while S did not show clear necessity. Therefore, each principle needs a different kind of future validation.

**中文翻译**

本研究有两个主要发现。第一，两项工具都已完成开发，并在实际研究流程中显示出可用性。第二，A/R/S 原则得到提出和测试。R 同时表现出必要性和有效性；A 尚未显示明确的有效性；S 尚未显示明确的必要性。因此，未来需要用不同方式继续验证各项原则。

## Slide 15 — Future Work

Next, I will complete a deeper analysis of the full A/B test data, including statistical analysis. I will also test the new sign prototypes with real visitors in the botanical garden and study their reading behavior, attention time, and satisfaction in a natural setting.

**中文翻译**

下一步，我将深入分析完整的 A/B 测试数据，包括统计分析。我还会邀请植物园中的真实游客试用新版标识原型，并在自然环境中研究他们的阅读行为、注视时间和满意度。
